About our company

Quality, coordination and time schedule — on every project

“Driven by continuous technological advancement and system upgrades, modern power distribution necessitates specialized expertise and superior product quality across various fields of MEP and Services Engineering.”

Best MEP Private Limited is a Mechanical, Electrical, Plumbing, ELV, BMS, Fire Fighting & Air Conditioning contracting company with efficient and dedicated engineering workers, serving several industries with design, supply, erection / installation, upgradation and commissioning of all Mechanical, Electrical, Plumbing, ELV, Fire Fighting & HVAC systems. Our systems and services are well adapted for hot, dusty, and humid environments.

We blend mechanized and modern methods of erection and installation with trained, experienced workers to deliver economical solutions. For the success of any project, three aspects are vital — quality, coordination and time schedule — and highly qualified project teams execute our services accordingly.

Our Contract Services Division delivers complex, deadline-driven projects covering the complete scope of design, supply, installation, upgradation and commissioning. We are committed to long-term client relationships, reflected in the repeat business from many of our key customers.

What we do

Our services

One contractor for the complete MEP scope — from design and supply to installation, testing and handover.

01

HVAC System

Complete installation and commissioning of heating, ventilation and air-conditioning systems as per project design.

View scope

Installation

  • Water / air-cooled chillers, VRV / DX systems with AHUs / FCUs
  • Cooling towers
  • Ductwork, exhaust fans and air handling units
  • VFDs, controls and sensors for temperature, humidity and air quality

Commissioning

  • Testing of heating, cooling and airflow
  • Air and water balancing
  • Controls & automation checks
  • Compliance, documentation and client training
02

Building Management System

Integration of automated controls that manage a building’s mechanical, electrical and electromechanical services.

View scope

Installation

  • Sensors, controllers, actuators & control panels
  • Integration of HVAC, lighting, fire safety, security and energy management
  • Wiring and networking of all BMS devices

Commissioning

  • Component and system-wide functional testing
  • Sensor calibration and subsystem validation
  • Facility team training, documentation & handover
03

ELV Systems

Setup and integration of extra-low-voltage systems (below 50V) for communication, safety and automation.

View scope
  • CCTV surveillance & access control
  • Fire alarm and detection systems
  • Public address & voice evacuation
  • Building automation & control
  • Structured cabling & data networks
  • Audio-visual systems & intercoms

Commissioning includes functional testing, communication verification, end-user training and certification of system readiness.

04

Fire Fighting & Fire Alarm

Design, supply, installation, testing and maintenance of fire protection and alarm systems.

View scope
  • Design & engineering to relevant codes and standards
  • Fire pumps, sprinklers, hydrants, hoses, extinguishers
  • Piping, sprinkler and hydrant system erection
  • Smoke / heat / flame detectors, call points, panels, sirens & strobes
  • Integration with BMS / emergency response
  • Testing, commissioning and periodic maintenance
05

Electrical Works

Erection and commissioning of power distribution from HT to final lighting.

View scope
  • 33KV transformers and HT panels
  • DG sets with auxiliaries
  • Electrical switchboards; Main, Sub, Relay DB and control panels
  • Overhead & underground electrical installations
  • Industrial bus ducts
  • Trays and trunking
  • Indoor and outdoor lighting systems
06

Plumbing, WTP & STP

Water supply, drainage and sanitary systems, plus water and sewage treatment plants.

View scope
  • GI / CPVC / PPR / HDPE pipe layout as per drawings
  • Transfer & booster pumps with control panels
  • Tank connections, hydrostatic testing
  • UPVC / cast iron soil & waste lines, traps, fixtures
  • WTP: raw water tank, PSF & ACF, softener / RO, chlorination / UV, control panel
  • STP: screen & grit chambers, equalization tank, filtration, disinfection, sludge dewatering
07

Civil Construction

Safe, durable and sustainable structures — from concept and feasibility to handover and maintenance.

View scope

Building construction

  • Residential, commercial and industrial buildings
  • RCC, steel or composite structures
  • Masonry, plastering, flooring, finishing, roofing, waterproofing

Infrastructure

  • Roads, pavements, drainage, culverts, retaining structures
  • Boundary walls, footpaths, kerb stones, parking & landscaping
08

Interior Designing

Functional, timeless spaces for residential, commercial and institutional projects.

View scope
  • Interior design & space planning
  • 2D & 3D visualization (AutoCAD, SketchUp, Lumion, Photoshop)
  • Material selection & colour coordination
  • Lighting & furniture design
  • Turnkey project execution
  • Client consultation, vendor & site coordination, budgeting
