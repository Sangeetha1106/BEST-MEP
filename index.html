Our Civil & Project Execution Roadmap

  1. Concept & Feasibility Topographic site analysis, environmental evaluation, preliminary architecture and capital budgeting
  2. Design & Estimation Detailed structural / MEP calculations, BIM coordination, shop drawings and itemized BOQ estimation
  3. Procurement & Mobilization Sourcing tier-1 approved equipment, certified raw materials, machinery mobilization and site setup
  4. Construction & Erection Civil groundwork, structural framework, MEP conduit laying, ductwork, busway and equipment erection
  5. Testing & Commissioning Hydrostatic testing, insulation resistance, air/water balancing, BMS telemetry and safety certifications
  6. Handover & Warranty As-built drawings, statutory approvals, operations manuals, client staff training and maintenance lifecycle
