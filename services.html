Our Core Services

Delivering high-performance mechanical, electrical, plumbing, civil and automation infrastructure tailored to international engineering standards.

01

HVAC System

Complete installation and commissioning of heating, ventilation and air-conditioning systems as per project design.

View scope

Installation

  • Water / air-cooled chillers, VRV / DX systems with AHUs / FCUs
  • Cooling towers & condenser water loops
  • Ductwork, exhaust fans and air handling units
  • VFDs, controls and sensors for temperature, humidity and air quality

Commissioning

  • Testing of heating, cooling and airflow
  • Air and water balancing
  • Controls & automation checks
  • Compliance, documentation and client training
02

Building Management System

Integration of automated controls that manage a building’s mechanical, electrical and electromechanical services.

View scope

Installation

  • Sensors, controllers, actuators & control panels
  • Integration of HVAC, lighting, fire safety, security and energy management
  • Wiring and networking of all BMS devices

Commissioning

  • Component and system-wide functional testing
  • Sensor calibration and subsystem validation
  • Facility team training, documentation & handover
03

ELV Systems

Setup and integration of extra-low-voltage systems (below 50V) for communication, safety and automation.

View scope
  • CCTV surveillance & access control
  • Fire alarm and detection systems
  • Public address & voice evacuation
  • Building automation & control
  • Structured cabling & data networks
  • Audio-visual systems & intercoms

Commissioning includes functional testing, communication verification, end-user training and certification of system readiness.

04

Fire Fighting & Fire Alarm

Design, supply, installation, testing and maintenance of fire protection and alarm systems.

View scope
  • Design & engineering to relevant codes and standards
  • Fire pumps, sprinklers, hydrants, hoses, extinguishers
  • Piping, sprinkler and hydrant system erection
  • Smoke / heat / flame detectors, call points, panels, sirens & strobes
  • Integration with BMS / emergency response
  • Testing, commissioning and periodic maintenance
05

Electrical Works

Erection and commissioning of power distribution from HT to final lighting.

View scope
  • 33KV transformers and HT panels
  • DG sets with auxiliaries
  • Electrical switchboards; Main, Sub, Relay DB and control panels
  • Overhead & underground electrical installations
  • Industrial bus ducts
  • Trays and trunking
  • Indoor and outdoor lighting systems
06

Plumbing, WTP & STP

Water supply, drainage and sanitary systems, plus water and sewage treatment plants.

View scope
  • GI / CPVC / PPR / HDPE pipe layout as per drawings
  • Transfer & booster pumps with control panels
  • Tank connections, hydrostatic testing
  • UPVC / cast iron soil & waste lines, traps, fixtures
  • WTP: raw water tank, PSF & ACF, softener / RO, chlorination / UV, control panel
  • STP: screen & grit chambers, equalization tank, filtration, disinfection, sludge dewatering
